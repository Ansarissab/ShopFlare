ShopFlare

A free online store that small businesses can run without paying monthly platform fees.

Why I built it

In early 2024 I started an online store with two partners. We got some sales, but the monthly ad and platform costs kept eating money we did not have yet. When a small $25 bill arrived that we could not pay, the store shut down.

That stuck with me. I kept asking: why is there no proper free option for tiny businesses just getting started? Every tool I found had a fee hidden somewhere. So I stopped looking and built one. ShopFlare is for people starting from zero, who want to sell online without platform fees taking a cut before they have even found their feet.

What it does

For shoppers

  • Browse products with colors, sizes and live stock
  • Fast search and a cart that remembers them
  • Pay by card, cash on delivery, bank transfer, WhatsApp, or in person
  • Track and cancel their own orders
  • Leave verified reviews and ratings
  • Get an email when a sold-out item is back
  • Install the store like an app and use it offline
  • Shop in English, French or Urdu (right-to-left)

For the shop owner

  • Add products and photos (auto-compressed on upload)
  • Manage orders, statuses and shipping tracking
  • Create discount coupons synced with payments
  • Approve reviews, answer FAQs, write blog posts
  • Built-in cash register for in-person sales
  • Change name, logo, colors, shipping and policies live, no developer needed
  • One-click themes and a built-in analytics dashboard

How it is built

Modern, popular tools, picked so the whole thing runs for free and stays easy to look after.

Next.js 16 + React 19 Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui Cloudflare Workers (frontend + API) D1 database R2 image storage KV cache Stripe payments Resend emails Web Push notifications Turnstile bot protection Zod validation TypeScript

Why it costs $0 to host

Everything runs inside Cloudflare's free tier, so there is no monthly server bill. Payments go through Stripe, which only takes a small cut when a real sale happens, never a flat fee. A small shop can launch, take orders, and pay nothing until it actually starts earning.
